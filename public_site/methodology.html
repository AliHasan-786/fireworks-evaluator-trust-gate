Trust Gate v1.0

Methodology and limitations

Evidence before automation.

The model comparison uses deterministic ground truth and guardrails. The separate trust gate compares those automated outcomes with blind human pass/fail labels and gives missed failures priority over average agreement.

What is established

Two authenticated Fireworks serverless routes produced 120 complete canonical records each. Generated evidence covers accuracy, ambiguity detection, JSON reliability, errors, latency, and estimated cost. Neither model met the versioned ambiguity guardrail.

What remains unknown

Banking77 is a public proxy, not an enterprise production distribution. No independent human labels exist yet, so evaluator-human agreement, failure recall, and leniency remain undefined. One labeler will not establish inter-rater reliability. No hosted evaluator or production job is claimed.

Decision boundary

PASS requires at least 30 validated labels, five human failures, 85% agreement, 90% failure recall, and no more than 10% leniency. A missing or modified label file is always INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
