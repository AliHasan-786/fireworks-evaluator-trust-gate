Trust Gate v1.0

Methodology and limitations

Evidence before automation.

The gate compares deterministic evaluator outcomes with blind human pass/fail labels and gives missed failures priority over average agreement.

What the pilot can establish

A 30-case packet can reveal directional leniency, strictness, and obvious rubric gaps. Bootstrap intervals expose uncertainty; subgroups below 30 and fewer than five human failures are explicitly labeled weak evidence.

What it cannot establish

Banking77 is a public proxy, not an enterprise production distribution. One labeler cannot establish inter-rater reliability. Configured prices are not current-price claims. No live model, judge, hosted evaluator, or Fireworks evaluation job is represented without saved metadata.

Decision boundary

PASS requires at least 30 labels, five human failures, 85% agreement, 90% failure recall, and no more than 10% leniency. A missing label file is always INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE.
