Trust Gate v1.0

Methodology and limitations

Evidence before automation.

The model comparison uses deterministic ground truth and guardrails. The separate trust gate compares those automated outcomes with blind human pass/fail labels and gives missed failures priority over average agreement.

What is established

Two authenticated Fireworks serverless routes produced 120 complete canonical records each. Generated evidence covers accuracy, ambiguity detection, JSON reliability, errors, latency, and estimated cost. Neither model met the versioned ambiguity guardrail.

What the blind pilot established

One non-expert family reviewer completed a quick informal pass over all 30 fingerprint-validated cases without seeing evaluator scores. Agreement was 76.7%; the evaluator caught all five human failures and had 0% leniency, but it false-failed seven human-accepted responses. The gate therefore failed its 85% agreement criterion.

What remains unknown

This was not a domain-expert study. Banking77 is a public proxy, not an enterprise production distribution. One labeler does not establish inter-rater reliability, and five human failures is a small recall denominator. No production deployment or customer result is claimed.

Decision boundary

PASS requires at least 30 validated labels, five human failures, 85% agreement, 90% failure recall, and no more than 10% leniency. Missing or modified evidence remains INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; complete evidence that misses a threshold is FAIL.
